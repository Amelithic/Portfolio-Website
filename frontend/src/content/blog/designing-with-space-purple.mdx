The colour of a site sets its mood before a single word is read. For this portfolio I wanted something that felt like space: deep, quiet, and a little magical.

I anchored the palette around a very dark purple background with violet and blue accents. Gradients run from violet through indigo to blue, echoing the colours of distant nebulae. Borders are thin and slightly translucent, keeping the interface light even in dark mode.

A clean, minimalist layout lets the colour breathe. The whole design relies on a handful of tokens defined once in CSS and reused everywhere, so switching between dark and light themes is a matter of changing variables.
